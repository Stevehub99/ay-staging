Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 June 2026

This application ("the App") is a private, internal automation tool used by its operator to create and manage advertising campaigns through the Meta Marketing API and related Meta Platform APIs. The App is not offered to the general public and does not provide a consumer-facing service.

Information We Access

Acting solely on behalf of the operator and the operator's own authorized Meta Business assets, the App accesses advertising and page data (such as ad accounts, ad campaigns, ad creatives, and Facebook Page details) strictly to automate the operator's own marketing workflows. The App does not collect, request, or store personal information from members of the public.

How Information Is Used

Data accessed via the Meta APIs is used only to perform the advertising-management tasks the operator initiates, such as creating ad creatives and configuring campaigns. Data is not sold, rented, or shared with any third party.

Data Retention

The App does not maintain a persistent database of Meta user data. Any data retrieved during an operation is held only transiently for the duration of that operation.

Data Deletion

Because the App stores no personal data, there is no stored personal data to delete. For any request related to data handled by this App, contact the operator at the email address below.
